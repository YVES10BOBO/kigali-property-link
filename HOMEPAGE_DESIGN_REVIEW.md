## 🎯 RECOMMENDATIONS

### **1. NAVBAR IMPROVEMENTS**

#### **A. "List Property" Button Text Options:**

**Current:** `+ List Property`

**Better Options (Ranked):**

1. **"List Your Property"** ⭐ BEST
   - More personal and inviting
   - Clear call-to-action
   - Professional tone
   - **Recommended!**

2. **"Add Your Listing"**
   - Direct and clear
   - Action-oriented
   - Good alternative

3. **"Post Property"**
   - Short and simple
   - Common in real estate
   - Good for mobile

4. **"Sell/Rent Property"**
   - Very clear purpose
   - Slightly longer
   - Good for clarity

5. **"List Property"** (Current - OK but could be better)
   - Simple but less personal
   - Works but not as inviting

**My Recommendation:** Use **"List Your Property"** - it's more personal, inviting, and professional.

---

#### **B. Navbar Layout Improvements:**

**Current Issues:**
- Two CTA buttons might compete for attention
- "Get In Touch" and "List Property" both prominent
- Could be cleaner

**Recommended Changes:**

**Option 1: Simplify (Recommended)**
- Keep "List Your Property" as primary CTA (orange button)
- Remove "Get In Touch" from navbar (move to footer or make less prominent)
- Or make "Get In Touch" a text link instead of button

**Option 2: Keep Both But Differentiate**
- "List Your Property" = Primary (orange, larger)
- "Get In Touch" = Secondary (outline style, smaller)

**Option 3: Mobile Optimization**
- On mobile: Show only "List Property" button
- Hide "Get In Touch" or move to menu
