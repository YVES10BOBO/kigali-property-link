## 📧 How to Contact Agencies

### **Step 1: Find Contact Information**

**What You Need:**
- Company name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Office address
- Manager/owner name (if possible)

**Where to Find:**
- Google search
- Company website
- Facebook page
- LinkedIn
- Phone directory

---

### **Step 2: Prepare Your Pitch**

**What to Say:**

**Email Template:**

```
Subject: Partnership Opportunity - Free Property Listings

Dear [Agency Name],

I hope this message finds you well.

My name is [Your Name], and I'm the founder of Kigali Properties, 
a new online property platform in Kigali.

I'm reaching out to propose a partnership opportunity that could 
benefit both our businesses.

PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL:

We would like to offer FREE property listings to [Agency Name] 
on our platform. In return, you would get:

✅ Free listings for all your properties
✅ More visibility for your properties
✅ Direct inquiries from potential customers
✅ Professional property presentation
✅ Analytics to track property performance

WHAT WE NEED:

- Permission to list your properties
- Property details and photos
- Your contact information for inquiries

BENEFITS FOR YOU:

- More customers see your properties
- More inquiries and leads
- Free marketing for your properties
- No cost to you

We're launching soon and would love to partner with [Agency Name] 
as one of our trusted partners.

Would you be interested in discussing this partnership opportunity?

I'm available for a call or meeting at your convenience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Phone Number]
[Your Email]
Kigali Properties
```

---

### **Step 3: Phone Call Script**

**What to Say:**

```
"Hello, my name is [Your Name]. I'm calling from Kigali Properties, 
a new online property platform.

I'm reaching out to [Agency Name] to propose a partnership opportunity.

We're offering FREE property listings to real estate agencies. 
This means you can list all your properties on our platform at no cost, 
and you'll get more visibility and more customer inquiries.

Would you be interested in learning more about this opportunity?

[If yes] Great! Can we schedule a brief meeting or call to discuss?

[If no] I understand. Would it be okay if I send you an email with 
more details? You can review it when convenient.

Thank you for your time!"
```

---

### **Step 4: In-Person Visit**

**What to Do:**
1. Visit agency office
2. Ask to speak with manager/owner
3. Introduce yourself
4. Present partnership opportunity
5. Leave business card/flyer
6. Follow up via email/phone

**What to Bring:**
- Business card
- Flyer about your platform
- Laptop/tablet to show website
- Partnership proposal document

## 🎯 Partnership Proposal Template

### **Create a Simple Document:**

**Title:** Partnership Opportunity - Kigali Properties

**Content:**

```
PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL

FOR: [Agency Name]

FROM: Kigali Properties

WHAT WE OFFER:

✅ FREE Property Listings
   - List unlimited properties
   - No monthly fees
   - No commission fees
   - Professional presentation

✅ More Visibility
   - Your properties appear in search
   - Featured placement options
   - Professional photos and descriptions

✅ Direct Inquiries
   - Customers contact you directly
   - Lead management system
   - Inquiry tracking

✅ Analytics
   - See how many views your properties get
   - Track inquiry performance
   - Data-driven insights

WHAT WE NEED:

- Permission to list your properties
- Property information (details, photos)
- Your contact information
- Partnership agreement

BENEFITS FOR YOU:

- More customers see your properties
- More inquiries = more sales/rentals
- Free marketing platform

HOW IT WORKS:

[Your Name]
[Your Phone]
[Your Email]
[Your Website]

```

## 📞 Contact Methods (Ranked)

### **#1: Phone Call** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Best)

**Why:**
- Personal connection
- Immediate response
- Can explain clearly
- Builds relationship

**How:**
- Find phone number
- Call during business hours
- Use phone script above
- Follow up with email

**Best Time:** 9 AM - 5 PM, Monday-Friday

---

### **#2: In-Person Visit** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Effective)

**Why:**
- Face-to-face connection
- Can show website
- Builds trust
- Professional impression

**How:**
- Visit agency office
- Ask for manager/owner
- Present opportunity
- Leave materials

**Best Time:** Business hours, avoid lunch time

---

### **#3: Email** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Good for Follow-Up)

**Why:**
- Professional
- Can include details
- They can review when ready
- Easy to track

**How:**
- Find email address
- Send partnership proposal
- Follow up if no response

**Best For:** Initial contact, follow-up

---

### **#4: Social Media** ⭐⭐⭐ (Good for Finding)

**Why:**
- Easy to find agencies
- Can message directly
- Less formal
- Quick response

**How:**
- Find agency Facebook/LinkedIn
- Send message
- Include partnership proposal

**Best For:** Finding agencies, initial contact
