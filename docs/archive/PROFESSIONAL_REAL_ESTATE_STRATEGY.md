## 📋 Essential Features for Your Website

### **1. Property Owner Self-Service Portal** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why it's essential:**
- Owners list their own properties (you don't need to find them)
- Owners update status when sold/rented (automatic updates)
- Owners upload their own photos (no copyright issues)
- Owners provide real information (authenticity guaranteed)

**What it includes:**
- Owner registration/login
- Owner dashboard to:
  - Add new properties
  - Edit existing properties
  - Mark as "Sold" or "Rented" (auto-removes from available listings)
  - Upload original photos
  - Update property details
  - View inquiries on their properties

**Benefits:**
- ✅ No need to travel - owners come to you
- ✅ Auto-status updates - owners mark sold/rented
- ✅ Real properties - owners verify ownership
- ✅ No copyright - owners upload their own photos
- ✅ Always accurate - owners maintain their listings
