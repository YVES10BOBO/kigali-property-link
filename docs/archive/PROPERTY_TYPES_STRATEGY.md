## 🎯 **RECOMMENDED STRATEGY: Phased Expansion**

### **Phase 1: MVP (Start Here) ✅**
**Focus: Residential Properties Only**

#### **Property Types:**
1. **Apartments** (1BR, 2BR, 3BR+) - Rent & Sale
2. **Houses/Villas** - Rent & Sale
3. **Studios** - Rent & Sale
4. **Penthouses** - Rent & Sale

#### **Why Start Here:**
- ✅ **Highest demand** in Kigali
- ✅ **Easier to verify** (clear ownership)
- ✅ **Standardized fields** (bedrooms, bathrooms, area)
- ✅ **Familiar to users**
- ✅ **Less complexity**

#### **Benefits:**
- Build user base quickly
- Establish credibility
- Learn from user behavior
- Perfect your verification system

---

### **Phase 2: Expand (After 3-6 months)**
**Add: Land & Commercial**

#### **New Property Types:**
5. **Land Plots** - Sale only
   - Residential plots
   - Commercial plots
   - Agricultural land
   
6. **Commercial Properties** - Rent & Sale
   - Shops/Stores
   - Offices
   - Warehouses

#### **Why Add These:**
- ✅ **High value transactions**
- ✅ **Growing market demand**
- ✅ **Different customer segment**
- ✅ **Higher commission potential**

#### **Special Fields Needed:**
- **Land:** Plot size, zoning, access roads, utilities
- **Commercial:** Business type, foot traffic, parking spaces

### **Phase 3: Advanced (After 6-12 months)**

#### **Why Add These:**

- Booking calendar system

## 🚫 **DON'T ADD YET (Postpone):**

### **❌ Hotels/Booking Rooms**
**Why Not Now:**
- Requires booking system (complex)
- Different business model
- Payment processing needed
- Calendar management
- Review system required
- **Better to add later** when you have more resources

### **❌ Land Plots (Initially)**
**Why Not Now:**
- Different verification process
- Requires different fields (zoning, surveys)
- Legal documentation more complex
- **Add after you master residential**
