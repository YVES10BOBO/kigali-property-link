# 💼 How Commission Flow Works: Buyer, Seller, and You (The Agent)

## 🎯 Your Role as an Agent

You're the **middleman** between:
- **Property Owner (Seller/Landlord)** = Homart Properties
- **Client (Buyer/Renter)** = Person looking for property
- **You (Agent)** = The connector who earns commission

---

## 📋 How the Process Works

### **Step 1: Client Finds Property on Your Website** 🌐

1. Client visits your website
2. Client sees a property (e.g., Greenland Plaza 2BR)
3. Client clicks "Book a Viewing" or "Inquire"
4. Client fills out form (name, phone, email)
5. **You receive the inquiry** → Saved in your dashboard

---

### **Step 2: You Contact the Client** 📞

1. You see the inquiry in your dashboard
2. You call/WhatsApp the client
3. You arrange a viewing appointment
4. You coordinate with Homart Properties (property owner)

**Example:**
- Client: "I want to see Greenland Plaza 2BR"
- You: "Great! Let me arrange a viewing for you. When are you available?"
- You: (calls Homart) "I have a client interested in viewing apartment #302"

---

### **Step 3: The Viewing Meeting** 🏠

**Two Scenarios:**

#### **Scenario A: You Attend the Viewing** ✅ (BEST)

1. You meet the client at the property
2. You show them the property
3. Homart representative may also be there
4. **You are present** → You can track the deal

**Why This is Best:**
- ✅ You're involved in the process
- ✅ You can track if the deal closes
- ✅ You can claim commission
- ✅ You build relationship with client

**Example:**
- You: "Let me show you around..."
- Client: "I love it! I want to rent it."
- You: "Great! Let me help you with the paperwork."

---

#### **Scenario B: Client Goes Directly to Homart** ⚠️ (RISKY)

1. Client finds property on your website
2. Client contacts Homart directly (bypasses you)
3. Client views property without you
4. Client signs lease/purchase
5. **You don't know about it** → You might not get commission

**Why This is Risky:**
- ❌ You're not involved
- ❌ You don't know if deal closed
- ❌ You might not get commission
- ❌ You did the marketing but didn't get paid

---

## 💰 How You Get Commission

### **Standard Process:**

1. **Client finds property on your website** → You get inquiry
2. **You arrange viewing** → You're involved
3. **Client decides to rent/buy** → Deal closes
4. **You notify Homart** → "This client came from my website"
5. **Homart pays you commission** → Based on your agreement

**Example:**
- Property rent: $800/month
- Your commission: 1 month rent = $800
- Or: 5% of sale price = $7,500 (if $150,000 sale)

---

## ⚠️ The Challenge: Deals Outside Your Website

### **Problem: Client Doesn't Come Through Your Website**

**Scenario:**
1. Client sees property on your website
2. Client remembers the property name/location
3. Client goes directly to Homart (bypasses you)
4. Client rents/buys without you knowing
5. **You don't get commission** ❌

**Why This Happens:**
- Client wants to avoid "agent fees"
- Client thinks they'll get better price
- Client doesn't understand your role
- Client finds property elsewhere (Google, Facebook, etc.)

---

## ✅ Solutions to Protect Your Commission

### **Solution 1: Track All Inquiries** 📊

**How It Works:**
- Every inquiry on your website is tracked
- You have proof the client came from your website
- You can claim commission even if client goes direct

**Example:**
- Inquiry #45: "John Doe - Interested in Greenland Plaza 2BR"
- John goes directly to Homart
- You show Homart: "This client came from my website (Inquiry #45)"
- You get commission ✅

---

### **Solution 2: Use Referral Codes** 🎫

**How It Works:**
- Each property gets a unique referral code
- Client must mention the code when contacting Homart
- Homart tracks which clients came from you

**Example:**
- Property: Greenland Plaza 2BR
- Referral Code: "KPL-GP-2BR-001"
- Client tells Homart: "I found this through Kigali Property Link, code: KPL-GP-2BR-001"
- Homart knows: This client came from you ✅

---

### **Solution 3: Agreement with Homart** 📝

**How It Works:**
- You have a written agreement with Homart
- Agreement states: "Any client who views property from Kigali Property Link website = commission to agent"
- Homart must report all deals to you
- You can verify if client came from your website

**Example Agreement:**
- "For any property listed on Kigali Property Link website, if a client views and rents/buys that property within 90 days of viewing, agent receives commission."
- This protects you even if client goes direct ✅

---

### **Solution 4: Require Registration** 🔐

**How It Works:**
- Clients must register/login to view property details
- You track which clients viewed which properties
- You have proof of client interest

**Example:**
- Client registers: "john@email.com"
- Client views: Greenland Plaza 2BR
- If John rents this property, you have proof he viewed it on your site ✅

---

### **Solution 5: Follow Up with Clients** 📞

**How It Works:**
- You contact every inquiry within 24 hours
- You build relationship with client
- Client is more likely to work through you

**Example:**
- Inquiry received: "John Doe - Interested in Greenland Plaza 2BR"
- You call: "Hi John, I saw your inquiry. Let me help you arrange a viewing!"
- John feels obligated to work with you ✅
